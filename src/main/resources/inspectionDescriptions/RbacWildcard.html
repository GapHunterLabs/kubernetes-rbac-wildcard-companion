Reports a Kubernetes Role or ClusterRole rule that combines a wildcard ("*") in verbs or resources with a sensitive resource: secrets, pods/exec or clusterrolebindings.

Overly permissive RBAC is the most common path from one compromised pod to the rest of the cluster (OWASP Kubernetes Top 10, K03). Both flow-style (["secrets"]) and block-style YAML lists are recognized.
